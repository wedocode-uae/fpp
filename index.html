03 · Customer strategy

From first contact to reliable operation.

Every enquiry follows a visible technical workflow that clarifies the requirement, controls execution and verifies the result.

STAGE 01

Enquiry and technical triage

We confirm the equipment, site, urgency and available information, then select the appropriate planned or emergency response route.

  • Equipment and system type
  • Site location and access
  • Fault symptoms or requested scope
STAGE 02

Site assessment and diagnosis

Technicians inspect the asset, record measurements, identify operational risks and distinguish the root cause from visible symptoms.

  • Condition assessment
  • Measurements and diagnostics
  • Immediate and lifecycle requirements
STAGE 03

Scope, method and proposal

The technical solution is converted into a clear scope with resources, materials, method, safety controls and proposed schedule.

  • Defined technical solution
  • Quotation and programme
  • Method and risk controls
STAGE 04

Execution and quality control

The field team completes the installation, repair or maintenance while controlling safety, workmanship and technical records.

  • Qualified mobilization
  • Controlled field execution
  • Progress and quality records
STAGE 05

Testing, handover and ongoing support

We verify operation, communicate recommendations and structure preventive support where continued maintenance is required.

  • Testing and commissioning
  • Clear handover documentation
  • Preventive maintenance or AMC
